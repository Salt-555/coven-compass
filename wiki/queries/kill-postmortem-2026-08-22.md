# Coven Compass — Kill Post-mortem (2026-08-22)

> **SUPERSEDED 2026-08-17/09-22.** The kill was reversed by Salt's 2026-08-17 directive
> (main target, keep on + modify) and the tool re-passed TIER 1 within 24h. The
> post-mortem's own prescription — bundle spell-tracking into a tarot-style tool —
> was implemented 2026-09-10: see [[bundle-merge-tarot-2026-09-10]] and
> [[pricing-history]]. Kept for the lessons below. Current state: [[coven-compass]].

## What was tested
Re-run of the coven-compass witchy spiritual tool (interactive spell/moon-phase tracker,
localStorage-persisted). Full-mode storefront with Stripe, price $12. TIER 1 re-run
(first-purchase test) — see [[coven-compass]] for the original hypothesis.

## Numbers
- Spend: $45.99
- Purchases: 2 (confirmed, $12 each)
- Revenue: $24
- CPA: $23 (=$45.99 / 2) — ABOVE the $12 price. Unit economics negative.
- Kill line: pre-set TIER 1 (CPA > price at window close) — FIRED.

## Verdict
KILLED (abandon-in-place), 2026-08-22. Meta campaign paused. Site stays live as a
portfolio artifact. This was a re-run — the first run also failed to clear its kill line.

## Creative angles tested
- (Re-run did not re-test new creative angles before the kill line fired — the pre-set
  line was hit within the window.)
- First-run learnings: witchy/spiritual angle sold to the same audience as tarot
  (r/tarot, witchy TikTok, Pinterest). Lower funnel than expected at $12 price point.

## Pricing anchors tried
- $12 — impulse-buy price for witchy audience, justified by spell-tracking feature
  (vs $29-30/mo subscriptions). Conversion read: 2/confirmed purchases on $45.99 spend
  ≈ 0.05% click-to-purchase — did NOT sustain at this anchor for cold traffic.

## Customer objections surfaced
- Same audience as tarot-reader, but the offer (spell tracking) did not convert cold
  traffic at the same rate. Implies the witchy niche is real (tarot sells) but this
  particular tool's value prop was weaker than the interactive reader.

## What we'd reuse if revisiting this space
- The audience is validated (tarot-reader's sister market). Don't re-test witchy tools
  cold without a sharper hook or a lower price anchor.
- If revisiting: bundle spell-tracking INTO a tarot-style interactive tool (same buyer,
  adjacent pain — see idea-miner bundling), or test at $7 to match the proven anchor.
- CHECK before mining adjacent: scoreboard already flags this topic's kill. Treat any
  new witchy-spiritual idea as a DOUBLE-DOWN-required, not an auto-green-light.
